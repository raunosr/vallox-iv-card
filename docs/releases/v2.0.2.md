# Vallox IV Card 2.0.2

Understand frequent post-heating without first setting an energy budget or waiting
for a change in your historical consumption.

## What changed

- The Energy details now show 24-hour heater runtime, separated into ordinary
  supply-air heating and heater use during defrost. They also show the whole unit's
  measured electricity during non-defrost heating and the average temperature
  difference between the core outlet and final supply air.
- Frequent heating is detected directly from six-hour state history. A consistently
  high consumption baseline or an empty daily budget no longer hides the observation.
  When a more efficient main heat source is configured, the card suggests manually
  comparing a lower supply-air target while monitoring electricity, room temperature
  and drafts. No target change is made automatically and no minimum is imposed.
- Heater runtime remains available without an energy meter. Missing or incomplete
  electricity readings are stated explicitly; whole-unit kWh is never described as
  heater-only consumption. Defrost heating alone does not trigger the supply-target advice.
- The editor explains optional thresholds and renames the baseline setting to
  **Deviation from your history**. The default `0.5` means more than +50% for three
  consecutive comparable hours, not an acceptable energy-use limit.
- Updated user documentation explains the measurements and the manufacturer/forum
  evidence behind heating and defrost observations.

## Update

Update **Vallox IV Card** to **v2.0.2** in HACS, then reload the dashboard in each
browser/tablet. If it is not listed, open the repository in HACS and choose
**⋮ → Update information**. Existing YAML remains compatible. In the editor's
Suggestions section, choose your main heating system; numerical limits are optional.

## Validation and limits

TypeScript, ESLint, 44 logic tests and 59 browser tests pass. Coverage includes meter
gaps/resets, unavailable states, heating/defrost separation, low user temperature
preferences, stable high consumption, compact layouts and both languages/themes.
The companion YAML is also checked by 13 deterministic Python tests.

The new winter scenarios are simulations, not real-device winter validation.
The optional seasonal/profile companions remain experimental. Keep heater functions
needed by the unit for defrost available. A reduction in Vallox electricity does
not by itself prove whole-home energy savings.
